Why This Order?

  • Account takeover causes most damage. Email + password manager + 2FA come first because your email inbox is the master key that resets every other account.
  • Updates close known holes. Most real-world compromises exploit bugs that were patched months earlier — automatic updates quietly remove that whole category.
  • Backups defeat ransomware. With a tested offline/cloud backup, a locked or stolen device is an inconvenience, not a catastrophe.
  • Habits beat tools. Phishing defeats strong passwords by tricking the human — verification habits are a security control too.
